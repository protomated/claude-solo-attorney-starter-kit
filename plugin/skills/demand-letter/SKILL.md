# /demand-letter — Demand Letter Generator

Draft a practice-area-specific demand letter from local matter files or intake notes. The attorney reviews and signs before anything leaves the office.

**This skill reads files only. It does not send anything.**

## Invocation

```
/demand-letter [matter folder path or client name]

```

---

## Workflow

### Step 1 — Collect matter context

Use the Filesystem connector to locate the matter folder. Look for:
- `intake.md` or `intake-summary.md` — parties, facts, injuries/damages
- Medical records summaries, repair estimates, or damage documentation
- Prior correspondence with the counterparty or insurer
- Any existing demand or negotiation history

If no matter folder is found or provided, ask the attorney to paste or describe the key facts:
- Parties (client name, counterparty name and address)
- What happened (incident date, facts of the claim)
- Damages or relief sought (dollar amount or specific action)
- Deadline the attorney wants to set

### Step 2 — Identify the practice area

Determine the type of demand from the facts and ask if unclear:

| Practice area | Key indicators |
|---|---|
| Personal injury | Accident, injury, medical bills, pain and suffering |
| Contract breach | Agreement, non-payment, non-performance, specific performance |
| Employment | Wrongful termination, discrimination, unpaid wages, harassment |
| Collections | Unpaid invoice, debt, promissory note |
| Property damage | Damage to property, repair costs, diminution in value |

### Step 3 — Draft the demand letter

Draft using the following structure, adjusting tone and legal theory by practice area:

```
[Attorney name and firm letterhead info from matter folder or ask]
[Date]

[Counterparty or insurer name]
[Address]

Re: Demand for [Compensation / Performance / Payment] — [Client name] v. [Counterparty]

Dear [Name or "Claims Representative" for insurers]:

This firm represents [Client name] in connection with [brief neutral description of the matter].

FACTS

[2–4 factual sentences: what happened, when, and who was involved. Neutral, accurate, sourced from files.]

LIABILITY

[1–3 sentences establishing the legal basis for the claim. Keep to the applicable theory: negligence, breach of contract, statutory violation, etc. Do not overreach.]

DAMAGES

[Itemized or summarized damages:
- Medical expenses: $[amount]
- Lost wages: $[amount]
- Property damage: $[amount]
- Other compensable damages: [description]
- Total damages: $[amount]]

DEMAND

[Client name] demands [dollar amount or specific relief] in full settlement of this claim. This demand expires on [date — 30 days from today unless attorney specifies otherwise].

If we do not receive [payment / written confirmation of performance] by [deadline], we will pursue all available legal remedies without further notice, including [filing suit / arbitration / reporting to applicable agency].

Please direct all future communications regarding this matter to this office.

Sincerely,

[Attorney name]
[Bar number if in matter folder]
[Firm name]
[Address]
[Phone]
[Email]
```

**Drafting rules:**
- State facts neutrally and accurately — only what is documented.
- Do not overstate damages. Include only items with documentation or a reasonable basis in the file.
- Match the deadline to what the attorney has specified or default to 30 days.
- For personal injury letters to insurers: omit the "filing suit" threat — use "pursue all available legal remedies."
- Do not include confidential client information beyond what is needed for the demand.

### Step 4 — Present draft and request confirmation

Show the full draft and note any gaps:

> "Here is the draft demand letter. I was missing [list any items not found in the matter folder — e.g., counterparty address, total damages figure]. Please fill in the bracketed sections before sending. Would you like any changes?"

### Step 5 — Save to matter folder (optional, with confirmation)

If the attorney wants to save the draft to the matter folder, ask:

> "Would you like me to save this as `demand-letter-draft-[date].md` in [matter folder path]?"

Do not write any file without explicit confirmation.
